# Privacy Policy for MOSMB Wedding 2027

**Last Updated: September 28, 2026**

## Overview

This privacy policy applies to SMS communications sent regarding our upcoming wedding event.

## Information We Collect

We collect and store phone numbers provided by guests for the purpose of sending event-related communications, including save-the-date announcements, invitations, and updates.

## How We Use Your Information

Your phone number is used solely to:
- Send wedding-related announcements and invitations
- Provide event updates and logistics information
- Contact you regarding the event

We do not sell, share, or disclose your phone number to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.

## SMS Program Details

MOSMB Wedding 2027 sends up to 6 text messages over 6 months. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to unsubscribe at any time.

## Your Rights

**Opt-Out**: You may opt out of receiving SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by contacting us at the address below.

**Data Deletion**: Upon request, we will delete your phone number from our contact list.

## Data Security

Your phone number is stored securely and is accessible only to the event organizers.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or wish to opt out of communications, please contact the MOSMB Wedding 2027 organizers at: ospsn101@gmail.com.
